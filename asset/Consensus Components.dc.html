Consensus · data primitives, components, decisions

Four primitives carry the product. Each one is drawn from the data by a stated rule.

1 · Dispersion field

the signature visual
{{ fieldBTC }}
Dark · Bitcoin Cash, one exchange holds 90%
{{ fieldBCH }}
  • x = % distance from the agreed price, linear across ±1.5% (±1% on mobile). Percent, not bp: this is the casual reader's chart.
  • height = √(volume share ÷ 25%), capped at full, floor 8%. Same scale for every asset, so BCH's Deepcoin is visibly louder than BTC's BTCC.
  • colour: sage within 0.5%, ochre 0.5–2%, vermilion beyond 2%, muted grey if CoinMarketCap discards it. Off-market strokes also get a dot cap, so colour is never the only signal.
  • off-scale: beyond the break marks the axis is log-compressed into a gutter, labelled with name and value. Outliers are shown, never clipped.
  • Tolerance band ±0.5% is sage at 9% opacity with dashed edges. Brass line = agreed price.
  • Hover/focus/tap on a stroke shows the tooltip (below). Motion: strokes rise from the baseline once on load, 600 ms, settle curve; none under prefers-reduced-motion.

CoinMarketCap is the fixed ink index; our reading is the brass pointer. Scale ±60 bp, tolerance ±25 bp (proposed: 2.8× the 9 bp median). The only place in the product that uses basis points, because the gaps are smaller than 0.1%.

A 10×10 grid of 12 px cells. Filled cells = round(largest share × 100). Fill is ink under 25%, ochre 25–49%, vermilion 50%+. The empty outlines are the rest of the market, so one voice drowning the others is read as area.

Judgement calls

Percent by default, bp in one place. Exchange distances are percent (−20.7%). Basis points appear only for the benchmark gap, where values are under 0.1%, explained once inline: "1 bp = 0.01%". No toggle.

Band word leads, number stays. "Reliable 88". The band is the honest resolution of a score that compresses to 87–92; the number is kept because it's what makes the BTC-below-XRP ranking discoverable.

Light by default. A measurement read on paper; also the fastest way to not look like the other fifteen entries. Dark follows the system setting and the toggle.

Current state leads, history follows. 65 readings are dots, not lines. The watchlist carries duration ("65 of 65 readings"), which is the useful part of history.

GitHub in the footer of every page and at the end of How this works, next to the list of what wasn't checked.
